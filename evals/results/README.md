## Reading future results

Each entry must identify its run, hypothesis IDs, protocol/version, workstream, task domain, Society Kernel and baseline versions, outcome/quality measures, costs, completion status, and limitations. Link the exact report, manifest, and underlying measurements. Distinguish comparable runs from different protocols and task populations.

Retain negative, mixed, failed, and incomplete outcomes with explicit labels. An automatic index refresh summarizes recorded evidence; it does not establish a broad verdict or alter the hypothesis register. The [evaluation reporting contract](../README.md#reporting-lifecycle) defines the future publication flow.
